INSTRUCTIONS

Objects
- Hook (Gray diamond)
- Fish (Colorful capsules)
- Mines (Black circles)

Object Behaviors
- Hook: The hook is effectively the player, you control its horizontal movement
  to avoid colliding with mines or fish for as long as you can. Vertical movement
  is automatic and constant once a run is started (the camera will match the hook's
  Y coordinate once the game starts). When the hook collides with a fish, it will
  begin rising vertically and catching any other fish it collides with. If the hook
  collides with a mine, it will begin rising without the ability to catch any fish.
  You can "burst" which temporarily speeds up movement, and will kill any fish you
  collide with, extending the current run but removing that fish from the game, 
  so you cannot catch it later. Colliding with a mine during a burst still ends
  the current run. Once the hook reached the top it will play a splashing sound
- Fish: Fish move horizontally across the screen, reversing direction once the edge
  is hit. They are spawned in at a range of random intervals, with a range of random
  speeds. Larger fish appear behind smaller fish so you can always see every fish on
  screen. When a fish is caught, it will attatch to the hook and stay attatched until
  the hook reaches the top of the screen.
- Mines: Mines stay in one place (although appear to move vertically due to the
  camera movement with the hook). They will play an explosion sound and animation
  when they collide with the hook ONLY during descent, the hook does not collide
  with mines while rising, so any caught fish are safe.

Controls
- Move: Horizontal only, A/D keys, Left/Right arrow keys, Left joystick (Xbox/Dualshock/Dualsense)
- Start descent: Space Bar, A button (Xbox Controller), Cross button (Dualshock/Dualsense)
- Burst: Space Bar, A button (Xbox Controller), Cross button (Dualshock/Dualsense)
- Buy bursts ($500): B key, B button (Xbox Controller), Circle button (Dualshock/Dualsense)

Scoring
- The player "scores" by catching fish. The value of each fish caught is added to the
  "Fish Bucks" total once the hook reaches the top.

Goals and Win/Lose Conditions
- The goal of the game is survival. Try to go as deep as possible in order to set a
  personal record and catch as many fish on the way back up as possible.
- Not so much a win condition, more a "didn't lose" condition, which is not hitting
  a mine on the way down.
- Lose condition is hitting a mine, losing out on all fish above as the hook resets,
  and potentially wasting bursts used to reach the current depth.
